# Global Dog Ownership Index — internal research notes

Review date: 2026-07-12. Version: Founding Research Edition 1.0.

## Process, acceptance and classification

The review began with World Bank, OECD and WOAH context, then used the competent government, regulator or transport operator for country-specific questions. Sources were accepted only when publisher, geography and category relevance could be stated. Travel records reuse Passport Planner authorities and URLs where practical.

World Bank, OECD and WOAH are intergovernmental; AVMA and AVBC are professional organisations; statutory veterinary registers are regulators; Trenitalia and National Rail are official transport operators; Cape Town is representative local-government evidence.

## Scoring and confidence cautions

Scores interpret practical barriers through published rubrics and do not rank countries. High confidence is reserved for direct climate, regulator, operator or travel-authority evidence. Moderate confidence identifies authoritative but partial coverage. Limited confidence is intentionally common for housing permission, local access, everyday practicality and comparable costs.

South Africa: Cape Town is not national evidence; transport, pet-rental and cost comparability remain gaps. United States: federal travel evidence is strong, while state and local variation prevents national access claims. Italy: Trenitalia and EU travel evidence are direct; municipal access and pet-permitting housing remain incomplete. United Kingdom: its four jurisdictions are not merged; Great Britain and Northern Ireland travel sources remain distinct. Australia: Victoria is representative state evidence; remote access and cross-state transport remain gaps.

## Rejected source patterns

- Commercial or affiliate cost blogs: unclear basket, geography or methodology.
- “Most dog-friendly” listicles: unsupported composite claims.
- Search snippets and AI summaries: not stable primary evidence.
- Unverified directories: unclear completeness or professional status.
- Duplicate secondary reporting: original authority preferred.
- Isolated city anecdotes: unsuitable for national conclusions.

## Future needs

Seek comparable pet-permitting rental evidence, operator inventories, emergency veterinary access measures and purchasing-power-adjusted dog-cost baskets. Future countries require all eight categories, source resolution, unique review and route QA before publication.
